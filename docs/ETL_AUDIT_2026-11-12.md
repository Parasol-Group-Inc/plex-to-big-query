Read-only. Window: November 2026 to date (through 11-12, ~15:30 MT) and the last 7 days.

**How it was checked:**
- **Data:** a Sonnet agent rebuilt each report from the raw tables.
- **Screens:** a Sonnet agent read Plex's live screens (`plex_live_screens.py --dump`, dry run,
  502 s, nothing written) and compared them with BigQuery.
- **Lag:** Claude checked the ODBC freshness logs and the invoice-date finding.

Same method as `docs/ETL_AUDIT_2026-11-10.md`. Raw extracts are in
`etl-manual/audit_2026-11-12/`, which is gitignored.

**Verdict:** the reports match their raw data exactly, and Plex's screens for every day up to 11-11.
The differences with Plex are:
- **ODBC's normal lag** (today's shipments and production after the 13:00 refresh).
- **One invoice-date basis difference:** INV0000114, $19,982.20, in November in the report and in
  October in Plex.
- **The known valuation difference** (D32): quantity × price against the invoiced amount.

No failures.

## Freshness and lag

- **All the raw tables involved were rewritten on 11-12.** Most at the 13:00 probe run (the first
  that re-extracts only changed tables), the rest at the 04:00 full run. Shipper_Status (4 rows)
  never changes.
- **The 13:01 MT load carried data about 1 h old:** orders to 12:04 MT, production to 12:09 and
  shippers to 11:44 (ETL freshness log). The agent first read these Mountain timestamps as UTC and
  reported an 8 h lag; that was wrong.
- **Label Design releases:** the screen has 63 lines and the report 62, all on the screen. The extra
  line is order 646 (po_line_key 16689699), which ODBC still holds at release status 3638 (ODBC lag).

## Decisions and proposed fixes (not made)

1. **Invoice date basis (new).** The report dates an invoice by when a shipper line was linked to it
   (`Shipper_AR_Invoice.Add_Date`), not by the invoice's own date (`AR_Invoice.Invoice_Date`).
   - Impact today: 1 invoice, $19,982.20, moves from October to November.
   - Proposed: `COALESCE(ar.Invoice_Date, inv.Add_Date)` in `reports/sql/shipping_revenue_report.sql`
     line 140.
   - Ask Jennilyn first: her scorecard views read this report.
2. **D32:** value invoiced lines at the invoiced amount, or add an invoiced column. The gap is now
   $21.9K for November.
3. **D27:** the 5-part BOM cleanup is still pending; 11,500 units are flagged.
4. **Weekly production update:** confirm its departments are meant to differ from the work-centre
   groups.

## Outcome (Jennilyn's answers, 2026-11-13)

1. **Invoice date basis: no change; D40 closed.** "We cannot change any financials for Oct because
   the reporting is done. In general, we report revenue based on shipments, not the invoices."
2. **D32: no change.** "Our revenue should be based on shipment quantity and shipment value.
   Invoicing may lag behind." The quantity × price basis stays.
3. **D27: the 5/9 parts are dropped again,** at any date, while the 9-part has an active 5-part BOM
   line. That is 11,500 units in November (3 parts). She says 5 parts are still on this setup.
4. **Weekly production update: our bug, fixed.** The departments did not differ by definition. The
   view reached the workcenter through `Part_v_Job_Op`, which missed 2,575 November records
   (Labeling 283,763 units). It now uses the record's own workcenter, and its MTD equals the monthly
   report. Her note: the sheets are largely moving to tracking in Plex, except Encapsulating and
   Bottling.
