Shipping Reporting Details: Jennilyn Tockstein advised using the shipping report to track the number of packages shipped, the count of orders shipped, and shipping revenue from the previous day. Inventory Risk Analysis Logic: Emilio Dominguez and Jennilyn Tockstein defined the "out of stock" logic for the inventory risk analysis report. Work in Progress Metric: The "work in progress" (WIP) metric was defined by Jennilyn Tockstein as any order line that is in a "pending fulfillment" status (00:26:17). Sales Month-to-Date Reporting: Jennilyn Tockstein clarified that "sales month-to-date" counts any order line that entered "pending fulfillment" status for the first time during the current month (00:33:40). The metric will also track the associated sales representative (00:36:14).

Pipeline Opportunities Forecast: The pipeline opportunities forecast will combine data from Monday.com with data from Plex. Specifically, it will sum Monday.com figures with Plex sales orders currently marked as "quote" or "pending sales approval" (00:38:27).
Production Actual versus Goal: Production data, specifically actual versus goal, will be organized by work center group and month. Jennilyn Tockstein indicated they will set up a goal table to manage this metric (00:38:27).
